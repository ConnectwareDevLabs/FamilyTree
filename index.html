🌳 About FamilyRoot & User Guide
🌳

FamilyRoot v2.2

Local-First, Privacy-Preserving Family Tree & Social Graph Builder

🔒 Privacy & Local Storage Architecture

FamilyRoot operates on a 100% local-first privacy model. All your family names, photos, birth dates, and relationship links are stored entirely within your browser's LocalStorage and IndexedDB. Your family data is never uploaded to any central server without your explicit sync request.

📖 How to Use FamilyRoot
1
Create Your First Family Member:

Click the ➕ Add First Member or topbar ⋯ button to start your tree with yourself or your ancestor.

2
Add Parents, Spouses, Children & Siblings:

Click on any member card on the screen to open the action menu. From there you can add parents, spouse, children, or siblings. Use the "Their Family" quick-add section to add an entire immediate family at once!

3
Switch Between Views:

Use the topbar switcher to toggle between 🌳 Hierarchy Tree (classic ancestral layout), 🕸️ Social Graph (interactive network diagram), and 📋 Member List (searchable directory).

4
Cross-Tree Person Directory & Reuse:

When adding new family members, type their name to see existing records from your other trees. Select a match to link the same person across multiple family branches!

5
Backup, Export & Share:

Export your tree as high-resolution PNG pictures, PDF documents, JSON data, or compressed binary .msgpack backups via the sidebar. Keep regular backups to protect your family memory.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Protections
Your Family Privacy is Sacred

FamilyRoot is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy. We believe your family genealogy, relationships, photos, and personal dates belong solely to you.

1. Local Storage Data Storage

All member names, photos, notes, and relationship links are stored entirely within your device's web browser local storage (LocalStorage / IndexedDB). No family data is transmitted to external servers during regular offline/standalone use.

2. Cookies & Local Preference Items

We use minimal essential local cookies and storage items to remember your active view preference, active family tree ID, theme preferences, and cookie consent choice.

3. Analytics & Advertisements

We display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdSense to support the maintenance of FamilyRoot. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to website domains.

4. Your Data Rights

You retain full ownership of your data. You can export your full data at any time via Export Data or Backup, or completely delete your trees from local storage at any time.
